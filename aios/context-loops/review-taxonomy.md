# Context Loop Review Taxonomy

Review edits are evidence, not instructions.

## Categories

- `style_preference`
- `missing_fact`
- `missing_relationship_context`
- `unsupported_commitment`
- `bad_source`
- `stale_context`
- `incomplete_retrieval`
- `task_misunderstood`
- `human_judgment_only`
- `one_off_exception`

## Interpretation Rules

- Deletion does not always mean failure.
- Sending unchanged does not always mean success.
- Large edits after no retrieval suggest incomplete retrieval, not necessarily a voice rule.
- Removed deadlines, meetings, deliverables, pricing, scope, or availability language suggest unsupported commitment risk.
- Rejected candidates should suppress repeated proposals unless new evidence materially changes the case.
