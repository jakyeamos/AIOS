AIOS product work should prioritize operator clarity, repeat use, and decision support.
Surface provenance, status, missing evidence, and next action instead of relying on decorative summaries.
Drilldowns should answer why a state exists and what would change it.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Important system state can be inspected without reading logs.
- Warnings and blockers are visually distinct and textually precise.
