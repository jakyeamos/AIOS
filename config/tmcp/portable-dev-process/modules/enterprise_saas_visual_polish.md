# Module: Enterprise SaaS Visual Polish

Module ID: `@module:enterprise_saas_visual_polish`

## Purpose

Adapt the visual polish pass for serious B2B, internal-tool, operations, knowledge, governance, analytics, and admin surfaces.

## Use When

- The UI is a SaaS app, dashboard, CRM, admin console, workflow tool, knowledge system, operational system, or enterprise product.
- The screen needs credibility, scanability, and repeated-use ergonomics.
- A dashboard or data page risks becoming a vanity metric grid.

## Rules

- Prefer dense but organized information over marketing-style whitespace.
- Use one dominant work region and one supporting region at most above the fold.
- Prefer tables, queues, timelines, feeds, lists, and detail rails over equal-weight card quilts.
- Present metrics with decision context. Counts without ownership, trend, freshness, exception, or next action are weak signals.
- Use metric strips with dividers before separate KPI cards when the metrics are peers.
- Align numerical columns right and use tabular numerals where possible.
- Keep operational metadata visible when it determines trust: owner, source, freshness, status, last update, permissions, and review state.
- Avoid oversized hero treatments inside everyday app screens.
- Avoid fake command-center drama, decorative chart grids, and generic "insights" panels.
- Settings and permissions should show domain grouping, impact, disabled-state reasons, and audit or recovery paths.

## Anti-Patterns

- Equal-weight dashboard card grids with no attention hierarchy.
- Total users, total documents, total departments, and other vanity counts as primary dashboard anchors.
- Decorative charts that do not support trend, comparison, distribution, or anomaly decisions.
- Search results rendered as cards when users need snippets, metadata, facets, and comparison.
- Toggle walls with no explanation of impact or scope.
- Centered product dashboards that feel like landing pages.

## Output Guidance

When asked for a boilerplate polish pass, keep recommendations portable. Do not inject a specific product's typography, colors, or domain metaphors unless a selected branch provides them.
