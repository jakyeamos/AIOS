## Direction

Framework Labs should read as a serious technology magazine with an engineer's monospace fingerprint: editorial calm, engineering precision, black-and-white restraint, and credibility from demonstrated substance.

## Visual System

- Use strict black on white as the identity. Do not soften the main canvas or ink with warm neutrals.
- Avoid chromatic accents. Use link blue only for inline body links, not buttons, backgrounds, badges, or decorative accents.
- Use square geometry for buttons, inputs, cards, and panels. Circular geometry is limited to icon containers or avatars.
- Use hairline dividers as the primary elevation system. Do not use shadows, glow, glass, gradients, or filled decorative panels.
- Keep display serif headlines at normal weight. Do not make the display face bold to create emphasis.

## Typography

- Use a high-contrast display serif for hero and section headlines.
- Use a readable body serif for article body, leads, and bylines.
- Use a sans face for navigation, buttons, captions, and structural UI.
- Use monospace only for eyebrows, section numbers, status lines, table headers, data cells, metadata, code, and measurement-like furniture.
- Never use monospace for narrative prose or headlines, serif for buttons or nav, or sans as the primary article voice when the editorial system is in scope.

## Layout And Copy

- Treat the page as a publication, not a funnel. Prefer mastheads, hero bands, story rows, bylines, diagrams, and article-like depth over feature grids, pricing cards, testimonials, logo walls, or sticky demo bars.
- Demonstrate rather than assert. Prefer diagrams, specs, written thinking, named people, caveats, and evidence to marketing claims.
- Use plain, declarative, present-tense copy. Avoid hype vocabulary, exclamation marks, and unsupported metrics.
- Keep motion restrained: one quiet page-load reveal at most, instant/minimal hover states, no parallax, scroll-jacking, or animated backgrounds.

## Output Guidance

When this branch is selected, call out which choices are Framework Labs-specific. If the screen is not a publication or research-lab surface, record this branch as skipped rather than forcing the identity onto the product.
