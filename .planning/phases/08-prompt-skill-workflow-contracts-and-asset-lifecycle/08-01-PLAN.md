<objective>
Establish the cross-asset lifecycle foundation for Phase 8: define one typed five-state literal shared by prompts, skills, and workflows; introduce AssetRecord and LifecycleTransition dataclasses; reuse promotion_lifecycle_items as the single lifecycle persistence table; extend the three asset registries to carry lifecycle_state, applicability, and purpose without breaking existing six workflows or five prompt templates; ship the migration that normalizes legacy status strings.

Purpose: ASSET-01 and ASSET-02 require canonical lifecycle vocabulary and persistence. Today route_status is prompt-only with two values; skills and workflows have no status field; promotion_lifecycle_items.status is freeform. Plan 01 establishes the shared primitives that Plans 02, 03, and 04 all depend on.

Output: services/asset_lifecycle.py (new), bin/migrate-lifecycle-statuses.py (new), extensions to the three registries with backward-compatible loaders, and a test suite covering the five-state literal, transition validation, migration idempotence, and writeback-before-promote gating.
</objective>
