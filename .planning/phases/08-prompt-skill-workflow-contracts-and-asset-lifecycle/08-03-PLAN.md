<objective>
Build evidence-backed asset recommendation that joins existing per-run evidence into a unified projection and wire it into the packet and route seams so prompts, skills, and workflows are recommended from durable evidence rather than hardcoded heuristics. Plan 03 introduces services/asset_recommendation.py, consumes it from services/agentize.py (replacing the hardcoded _standards / _success_criteria / relevant_skills lists with recommender output and labeled fallbacks), and extends services/task_routing.py RouteResult with skill_recommendations and workflow_alternatives populated by the recommender. The prompt_recommendation rationale enrichment (Anti-Patterns rule) is performed inside start_route as a post-hoc decoration of the dict returned by recommend_route_primitives — Plan 03 does NOT modify services/workflow_orchestration.py, which keeps Plan 03 file-disjoint from Plan 02 and lets both run in Wave 2.

Purpose: ASSET-03 requires links between assets and the workflows/task families where they succeeded or failed. ASSET-04 requires that those links drive recommendations during packet generation and handoff creation. Plan 03 builds both layers in parallel: the projection layer that establishes the links, and the consumption layer that surfaces them. Pitfall 3 (greedy lock-in) is mitigated by an exploration-mode rule below sample_size threshold 10.

Output: services/asset_recommendation.py (new) with two dataclasses and three public functions; extensions to services/agentize.py and services/task_routing.py; three test files (one new, two extended) covering aggregation, ranking, fallback, and consumer integration.
</objective>
