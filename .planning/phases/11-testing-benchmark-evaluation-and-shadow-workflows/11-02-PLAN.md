<objective>
Create the second brain evaluation track: retrieval logging, gold-set context tests, precision/recall/staleness metrics, and the ablation policy files for second-brain variants. This is the instrumentation that lets AIOS prove Second Brain Lift (full second brain vs repo-only) on specific known tasks.

Purpose: The Phase 10 templates define Second Brain Lift conceptually. This plan creates the infrastructure to measure it. The gold-set task pattern ensures the recall measurement is deterministic (we know which sources were needed) rather than subjective.

Output: schema.sql extension (2 new tables); services/second_brain_eval.py NEW; 4 ablation policy JSON files; services/aios_cli.py extension (3 subcommands); test coverage.
</objective>
