<objective>
Ship the Python-side explainable delta contract (DELT-02), the four-state provenance classification (DELT-03), and the workflow-from-health recommender (DELT-04) without inventing new persistence — everything projects from existing rows.

Purpose: DELT-02 requires every score to surface evidence, confidence, freshness, contradiction, and remediation. DELT-03 requires confirmed/inferred/missing/contradictory classification. DELT-04 requires a prioritized backfill path and workflow recommendations. All three can be satisfied by projecting columns that already exist on `standards_assessments`, `standards_delta_items`, `standards_backfill_tasks`, and `success_criteria_findings` — no schema changes. Workflow recommendations are advisory; the recommender returns `available_in_registry: false` for planned workflows (`standards backfill`, `audit-only`, `codebase architecture review`, `security review`) so UI consumers in plan 07-03 can render them distinctly without trying to launch them.

Output: a `DeltaExplanation` frozen dataclass, `build_explanation()`, `_classify_provenance()`, `_detect_contradiction()`, `_findings_by_criterion()`, and `project_delta_explanations()` in `services/standards_health.py`; a `HEALTH_TO_WORKFLOW_RULES` table plus `recommend_workflow_from_health()` in `services/workflow_orchestration.py`; and tests covering each contract.
</objective>
