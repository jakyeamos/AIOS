# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the local operator responsible for AI-assisted development
across multiple repositories. They use AIOS while starting work, routing an
objective, monitoring execution, verifying evidence, reviewing gated effects,
and closing out the run with durable next actions.

## Product Purpose

AIOS is a local-first operating layer for serious AI-assisted project work. It
turns intent into a governed, inspectable run with explicit evidence,
approvals, unresolved risks, and closeout state. Success means the operator can
understand what is happening, why AIOS made a decision, what still needs human
judgment, and what to do next without assembling context manually.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The interface should feel like an expert
instrument for consequential work: clear about state and authority, dense when
the task requires it, and quiet when no action is needed.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative analytics walls, novelty navigation,
and opaque automation that hides its evidence or approval boundary. Do not
optimize for visual novelty at the expense of familiar controls, readable
status, or a direct path through the operating loop.

## Design Principles

- Make the next responsible action obvious.
- Show evidence, provenance, authority, and unresolved risk at the point of
  decision.
- Keep the local human approval boundary visible and unambiguous.
- Prefer one coherent operating loop over a collection of peer-level tools.
- Use familiar, quiet interaction patterns so attention stays on the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All core flows must work with keyboard navigation, visible
focus, semantic controls, programmatic names and states, sufficient contrast,
responsive layouts from 375px upward, and a reduced-motion preference. Color
must not be the only channel for status or authority.
