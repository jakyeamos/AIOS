# Overconfident Style Drift In Automated Writing

## Abstract

This paper studies style drift that appears when automated writing systems make cautious source material sound more certain than the evidence supports.

## Introduction

Humanizer tools can improve prose, but they can also remove the hedges and scope limits that make a research draft honest. The problem is not only factual hallucination. It is also rhetorical exaggeration.

## Method

The fixture contains claims with uncertainty markers and bounded conclusions. A rewrite is scored on whether it preserves these boundaries while improving clarity.

## Discussion

Useful style changes include shorter sentences, clearer topic transitions, and removal of filler. Harmful changes include adding certainty, broadening claims, or removing methodological caveats.

## Conclusion

Humanization should be treated as a constrained transformation with meaning-preservation checks, not as a general polish step.
